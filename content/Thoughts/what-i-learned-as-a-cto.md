After graduating I worked as CTO at a fintech [startup](https://triljon.com/). Here are a few things I took with me from the role.

### Things move fast

There was always something going on: a new design to implement, a suggestion for the stack to look into, or a feature to plan. That pace kept the work interesting and taught me to prioritize quickly.

### Communication matters

We had daily standups and good communication within the team. It's one of the most valuable things I learned, and something I've brought with me since.

### More responsibility than I expected

I was surprised by how many decisions were mine to make. I always researched options before suggesting a direction, which made those decisions easier to stand behind.

### Write things down

If I did it again, I'd document more. I rarely wrote down my research and suggestions, and notes would have made it much easier to keep track of why things were decided.

### If you get the chance

If you're offered a role like this, take it. It's a lot of fun, and there's always something exciting to work on.
